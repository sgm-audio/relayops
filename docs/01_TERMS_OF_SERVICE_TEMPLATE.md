## 1. Agreement structure

These Master Terms of Service (“Terms”) govern services provided by **[AGENCY LEGAL NAME]** (“Agency”) to the person or entity identified as client in a statement of work (“Client”).

The complete agreement consists of, in descending order of priority:

1. a signed change order, for the subject of that change only;
2. the applicable signed statement of work (“SOW”);
3. any service schedule referenced by the SOW;
4. these Terms; and
5. an accepted proposal, to the extent it does not conflict with the documents above.

The parties may accept documents by electronic signature. A Client representative who signs or approves a document represents that they have authority to bind Client.

## 2. Services

Agency provides only the deliverables expressly listed in an SOW. Services may include:

- website strategy, design, development, migration, integration, and support;
- Shopify design, theme work, configuration, migration, application integration, and support;
- Amazon catalogue, listing, content, brand-store, and operational support; and
- other services specifically added by a signed SOW.

**Audio Software is not an active service lane during initial testing** unless Agency and Client sign a separate pilot SOW and software/service schedule reviewed for licensing, support, privacy, security, and warranty requirements.

## 3. Paid discovery

### 3.1 Separate engagement

Discovery is a separate, fixed-fee advisory engagement. It may include interviews, current-state review, requirements, solution options, risk identification, scope, estimates, and implementation recommendations.

### 3.2 Start condition

The discovery fee and applicable taxes are payable in full before discovery is scheduled or begun. Agency has no obligation to reserve a date, review systems, configure accounts, produce recommendations, or begin any other discovery work before cleared payment and acceptance of the discovery SOW.

### 3.3 Not implementation

Unless a discovery SOW expressly says otherwise, discovery does not include production design, code, store configuration, account changes, listing changes, data migration, deployment, or implementation.

### 3.4 Conversion

Implementation requires a separate accepted SOW. The discovery fee is not credited against implementation unless the implementation SOW expressly provides a credit.

## 4. Mandatory implementation start gate

**Agency will not begin implementation work unless and until all of the following have been verified:**

1. paid discovery is complete, unless Agency expressly waives discovery in writing;
2. Client has approved the implementation scope, assumptions, exclusions, acceptance criteria, and dependencies;
3. an authorized representative has signed the applicable SOW and these Terms; and
4. the implementation deposit and applicable taxes have cleared Agency's payment processor or financial institution.

“Begin” includes scheduling production capacity, design or code production, account configuration, listing changes, content loading, migration, deployment, and deliverable preparation. General sales discussion and administrative receipt of Client materials are not implementation work.

An estimated start date is not reserved until the four controls above are complete. Agency may assign the next available start date after clearance.

## 5. Fees, invoices, and taxes

### 5.1 Default implementation schedule

Unless an SOW says otherwise:

- **50% implementation deposit** is due after discovery and before scheduling or production;
- **25% milestone payment** is due when the staging, draft, or equivalent review milestone is presented; and
- **25% final payment** is due before launch, transfer, credential release, or final handoff.

### 5.2 Invoice due date

Invoices are due within **7 calendar days** of issue. An SOW may require immediate payment for deposits, rush work, third-party purchases, or reinstatement.

### 5.3 Taxes and currency

Fees are stated in Canadian dollars unless expressly stated otherwise. GST/HST/PST/QST and other applicable taxes are additional. Client is responsible for bank, foreign-exchange, and payment-provider fees charged to Client.

### 5.4 Deposits and committed capacity

To the extent permitted by law, the implementation deposit compensates Agency for reserving capacity, onboarding, and early project commitments and is not refundable after the agreed start date. If Client cancels before the start date, Agency will account for work performed and non-cancellable commitments and apply the remainder as required by applicable law and the SOW. Counsel should tailor this clause to applicable consumer-protection law.

### 5.5 Late payment and suspension

Agency may pause work and withhold launch, transfer, credentials, source files, or handoff while an amount is overdue. Project dates move by at least the length of the pause and may be rescheduled to Agency's next available capacity. Interest on overdue business invoices is **[X% per month, X% annually]**, or the maximum lawful amount if lower, only after counsel approves the rate and disclosure.

## 6. Scope and change control

### 6.1 Included work

Only items expressly listed as deliverables are included. Estimates, examples, discussions, sales demonstrations, mockups, and references do not add scope.

### 6.2 Exclusions

Unless an SOW expressly includes them, the following are excluded:

- copywriting, translation, photography, video, audio production, legal review, accessibility certification, or tax advice;
- third-party subscription, app, marketplace, hosting, font, theme, plug-in, transaction, or advertising fees;
- data cleansing, manual product entry, or content entry beyond stated quantities;
- platform appeals, account reinstatement, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed sales, or policy exceptions;
- post-handoff maintenance, monitoring, content updates, and support; and
- new work caused by third-party changes after acceptance.

### 6.3 Change request

Either party may propose a change. Agency will identify the effect on fees, schedule, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Agency does not begin changed work until an authorized Client representative accepts a written change order and pays any required amount.

### 6.4 Emergency request

Agency may act on an urgent written request without a complete change order only when the parties confirm the hourly rate, spending cap, and authorized requester. Agency is not obligated to accept emergency work.

## 7. Schedule and Client dependencies

### 7.1 Schedule

Dates are estimates unless an SOW expressly labels a date as fixed. A milestone depends on timely Client decisions, complete materials, access, and third-party availability.

### 7.2 Client representative

Client will appoint one authorized project owner who consolidates feedback and approvals. Conflicting feedback does not extend Agency's included revision allowance.

### 7.3 Response times

Unless the SOW says otherwise, Client will provide requested feedback, access, content, and approvals within **3 business days**. Delay automatically moves dependent dates. Agency may reassign capacity after **10 business days** of inactivity.

### 7.4 Restart

After **15 business days** of Client-caused inactivity, Agency may close the active production slot and require a restart plan and reasonable restart fee stated in a change order. Amounts already due remain due.

## 8. Client responsibilities and warranties

Client will:

- provide accurate, complete, lawful materials and instructions;
- maintain control of its primary platform, domain, financial, and marketplace accounts;
- obtain rights, notices, and consents required for Client content and personal information;
- review deliverables and test material business workflows before approval;
- make business, legal, tax, shipping, product, advertising, accessibility, and platform-compliance decisions;
- maintain backups after handoff; and
- comply with applicable law and third-party terms.

Client represents it has sufficient rights to materials and access it supplies. Client will not ask Agency to use deceptive reviews, false claims, prohibited scraping, credential sharing that violates platform terms, infringing content, or unlawful outreach.

## 10. Review, revisions, and acceptance

### 10.1 Review rounds

The SOW states included review rounds. Feedback must be consolidated and relate to agreed scope. Additional or fragmented rounds are a change.

### 10.2 Defect notice

Client must describe a claimed non-conformity with steps to reproduce it and the acceptance criterion it fails. Preferences and new requirements are not defects.

### 10.3 Acceptance

A deliverable is accepted on the earliest of:

- Client's written approval;
- Client's production publication, commercial use, or transfer to another provider; or
- five business days after delivery for review if Client has not reported a specific, material failure against an acceptance criterion.

This deemed-acceptance clause must be reviewed for Client type and applicable law.

## 11. Handoff and warranty

### 11.1 Handoff condition

Final launch, ownership transfer, source-file release, credential transfer, and handoff occur only after final payment clears, unless the SOW expressly states otherwise.

### 11.2 Package

The handoff package includes only items listed in the SOW and the applicable handoff checklist. Client must confirm receipt, access, named ownership, known limitations, and training attendance.

### 11.3 Limited defect warranty

For **30 calendar days** after final handoff, Agency will correct reproducible defects that materially fail agreed acceptance criteria, subject to reasonable access. The warranty excludes:

- new features or changed requirements;
- Client or third-party modifications;
- unsupported environments;
- platform, browser, API, policy, application, theme, or vendor changes;
- security incidents not caused by Agency's breach; and
- Client's failure to follow handoff instructions.

Support after this period requires a separate agreement.

### 12.2 Agency background materials

Agency retains its pre-existing and independently developed tools, processes, know-how, templates, generic code, design systems, utilities, and reusable components (“Background Materials”). Agency grants Client the licence stated in the SOW to Background Materials embedded in paid final deliverables.

### 12.3 Final deliverables

After full payment, Client receives the ownership or licence rights expressly stated in the SOW to selected final deliverables. Unselected concepts, working files, internal tools, and materials outside the SOW are not transferred.

### 12.4 Third-party materials

Open-source software, fonts, stock assets, applications, themes, plug-ins, APIs, and marketplace content remain governed by their own licences. Client is responsible for ongoing licence fees after handoff.

### 12.5 Portfolio

After public launch, Agency may identify Client and display non-confidential final work in its portfolio unless the SOW prohibits it. Agency will not disclose confidential analytics or unpublished information.

## 13. Confidentiality, privacy, and security

Each party will use reasonable safeguards to protect the other's non-public business information and use it only for the agreement. Exceptions include information lawfully known without restriction, independently developed, public without breach, or lawfully received from another source.

Client will not supply sensitive or regulated personal information unless the SOW identifies the information, purpose, safeguards, locations, and deletion method. Agency may use vetted subprocessors to provide the services and remains responsible as required by applicable agreement and law.

Each party will promptly notify the other of a suspected security incident materially affecting the other's data and cooperate on containment and legally required notices. Neither party may make a public statement naming the other without consultation unless legally required.

## 14. Third-party services and force majeure

Agency is not responsible for failure, delay, suspension, policy change, price change, data loss, or discontinuation caused by a third-party platform outside Agency's reasonable control. Agency will reasonably assist with mitigation as included in the SOW or under a change order.

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, excluding payment obligations. The affected party will notify the other and use reasonable efforts to resume performance.

## 15. Termination

### 15.1 Material breach

Either party may terminate an SOW for material breach not cured within **10 calendar days** after written notice, or immediately for unlawful instructions, serious security risk, insolvency, harassment, or repeated non-payment where permitted by law.

### 15.2 Client convenience

Client may terminate for convenience on written notice. Client will pay for work completed, reserved/committed capacity as permitted, approved expenses, non-cancellable commitments, and wind-down/transfer work. The treatment of deposits is governed by section 5.4 and the SOW.

### 15.3 Effects

On termination, Agency will stop work safely, invoice amounts due, and—after payment—provide completed paid-for deliverables in their then-current state if technically and legally practical. Agency may remove its access, revoke temporary credentials, and return or delete information under the retention schedule.

Except for express warranties in the agreement and warranties that cannot be excluded by law, services and deliverables are provided “as is.” Agency does not guarantee revenue, profit, conversion, ranking, traffic, search placement, account approval, platform uptime, policy outcome, or uninterrupted operation.

Client acknowledges that digital services involve changing browsers, devices, APIs, platforms, applications, policies, and threats.

## 17. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

- neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential loss, or lost profit, revenue, data, goodwill, or opportunity; and
- Agency's aggregate liability arising from an SOW will not exceed fees paid to Agency under that SOW during the **three months** before the event giving rise to the claim.

The agreement must state any carve-outs required by law or negotiated for confidentiality, privacy, intellectual-property claims, fraud, wilful misconduct, or indemnity. Counsel must review this section.

## 18. Indemnities

Client will defend and indemnify Agency against a third-party claim arising from Client materials, products, instructions, legal/policy violations, or account activity, except to the extent caused by Agency's breach. Counsel must tailor procedure, control of defence, and caps.
