## 2. Objectives

- document the current state and desired business outcome;
- identify users, requirements, constraints, dependencies, and decision-makers;
- reduce technical, platform, content, migration, policy, privacy, and schedule uncertainty;
- define a prioritized implementation scope and explicit exclusions;
- create measurable acceptance criteria;
- provide an estimate range, phased delivery plan, and material risk register.

## 3. Included activities

- one 60–90 minute stakeholder kickoff;
- review of the completed discovery questionnaire;
- review of up to [NUMBER] identified systems/accounts and [NUMBER] pages/SKUs/listings;
- one current-state audit appropriate to the selected service lane;
- requirements and dependency workshop;
- solution and risk analysis;
- preparation of the discovery brief;
- one 45–60 minute findings presentation; and
- one consolidated clarification round delivered within two business days.

## 4. Service-lane module

Select one and delete the others before signature.

### A. Web Development

Review may include information architecture, CMS, hosting, domain/DNS, integrations, forms, analytics, search, content readiness, performance, accessibility target, security assumptions, migration, and browser/device support.

### B. Shopify

Review may include store plan, theme, applications, catalogue, variants, subscriptions, customers, orders, Markets, B2B/wholesale, POS, payments, shipping, taxes, discounts, analytics, domain, data migration, and owner/staff access.

### C. Amazon

Review may include Seller/Vendor context, marketplace(s), account roles, Brand Registry, catalogue, parent/child structure, listing quality, assets, A+ content, store, fulfilment, inventory dependencies, cases, account-health risks, advertising handoffs, and policy constraints. Discovery is not legal or policy advice and does not guarantee platform outcomes.

## 5. Deliverables

Agency will deliver:

1. **Discovery brief** — current state, desired outcome, users, constraints, and success measures.
2. **Prioritized requirements** — must/should/could/not-now classification.
3. **Scope map** — included deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, dependencies, and Client responsibilities.
4. **Risk register** — material technical, platform, content, data, policy, privacy, and schedule risks.
5. **Acceptance criteria** — observable tests for each proposed implementation deliverable.
6. **Implementation recommendation** — phases, schedule range, fee estimate/range, payment milestones, and proposed team.

Editable source/working notes are not deliverables unless expressly listed.

## 6. Not included

Discovery does not include:

- production design, development, configuration, content changes, listing changes, migration, deployment, or launch;
- legal, tax, accounting, security certification, accessibility certification, or Amazon policy advice;
- third-party purchases or vendor support fees;
- more systems, records, workshops, or revision rounds than stated above; or
- a guarantee that Agency will offer or Client will purchase implementation.

## 7. Client inputs

Client will provide before kickoff:

- a completed questionnaire;
- business objectives, target date, budget range, and success measures;
- one authorized project owner and required stakeholders;
- read-only or least-privilege access where review is required;
- existing analytics, architecture, catalogue, app/integration, policy/case, and content information;
- known legal, privacy, security, accessibility, marketplace, and contractual constraints; and
- consolidated feedback within two business days.

Agency may pause discovery until required inputs are complete.
